Steady Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026

Steady is an Android app that helps you cut down on drinking. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. The short version: everything you enter stays on your phone. We do not run any servers, and we never receive your data.

Signing in with Google

Steady asks you to sign in with your Google account. When you do, Google shares your name, email address and Google account ID with the app. Steady uses them only to show which account is signed in and to keep each account's entries separate on the device. They are stored on your phone and are not sent to us or to anyone else.

The sign-in itself is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

What you record

Drinks, urges, situations, thoughts, feelings, daily limits and plans are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. They are never uploaded, synced or shared. Because of that, we cannot see them or recover them. If you uninstall the app or lose your phone, the data is gone.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Deleting your data

In the app, tap the round account button at the top right of any screen, then tap Delete my data. That removes all of your entries from the phone and signs you out. Uninstalling the app also deletes everything. To remove Steady's access to your Google account, visit your Google account connections.

Children

Steady is intended for adults of legal drinking age and is not directed at children.

Not medical advice

Steady is a self-help tool and does not provide medical advice. If you drink heavily every day, talk to a doctor before cutting down, because stopping suddenly can cause dangerous withdrawal.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
